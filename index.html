I'm Thejovati Narayanan. I'm a soft spot for the people I love, a little stubborn about what I believe in, and firm about one thing: kindness and self-respect should always go together.

I'm from Chennai, so good food, the sea, and a little chaos feel like home. Growing up across different schools and countries taught me to adapt, embrace new places, and carry a little piece of home wherever I go.

I'm happiest around water, exploring new places, finding perfumes that feel like memories, or sharing good food. I'm just as up for a spontaneous “let's go somewhere” as I am for a quiet evening rewatching Suits for the fourth time. I love a little adventure, but I also love the comfort of knowing exactly how my favourite story ends.

Yoga has taught me patience and discipline, and winning national-level competitions remains one of my proudest experiences. I'm not always the loudest in the room, but I'm comfortable with who I am.

Currently, I'm pursuing an integrated B.Tech + MBA in Data Science at NMIMS MPSTME, Mumbai. But beyond what I study, I'm someone who wants a life filled with meaningful people, beautiful places, unforgettable flavours, and little moments worth holding on to.
